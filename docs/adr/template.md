# NNNN. Short title in the imperative ("Use X for Y")

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who made the decision
- **Supersedes:** [NNNN](NNNN-title.md) or —
- **Superseded by:** [NNNN](NNNN-title.md) or —
- **Commits:** short hashes that put the decision into the code

## Context

What problem or force makes a decision necessary? State facts, not the solution.

## Decision

What we do, in one or two short paragraphs. Use "we will" or the present tense.

## Considered options

- **Chosen option**: one line on why.
- **Alternative**: one line on why not.

## Consequences

### Good

- What becomes easier or safer.

### Bad

- What becomes harder, costs more, or is left open. Link open problems in [KNOWN_ISSUES.md](../KNOWN_ISSUES.md).

## Links

- Code, docs and related ADRs.
